    30-Second Quick Start

    • 1

      Everyone secretly places a card face down — three roses and one skull in your hand.

    • 2

      On your turn: add another card, or start bidding. A bid is how many cards you swear you can flip with zero skulls.

    • 3

      Highest bidder flips. Your own stack first, top-down, then raid anyone. Hit your number and you score. Hit a skull and you lose a card forever.

      Bid 3 → flip your own rose, your own rose, then Sam's top card … SKULL. You lose a card.

    Two successful challenges wins. Lose all four cards and you're out.

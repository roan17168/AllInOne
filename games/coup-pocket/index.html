    30-Second Quick Start

    • 1

      You may claim ANY role — even one you don't hold. That's the whole game.

    • 2

      Anyone can challenge your claim. Caught lying? You lose a card. Challenged wrongly? They lose a card.

      You claim Duke for Tax. Sam challenges. You really have Duke → Sam loses a card and you swap for a fresh one.
    • 3

      Last player with any influence wins. At 10+ coins you must Coup.

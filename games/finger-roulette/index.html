30-Second Quick Start

  • 1

    Put the phone flat on the table. Everyone presses one finger anywhere on the screen.

  • 2

    Hold still. Once everyone is down, the ring starts charging and the pitch climbs.

    4 fingers down → charge builds for ~3s → the blue finger is chosen → blue does the dare.
  • 3

    Lift early and it resets. No takebacks, no re-rolls, no negotiating with the phone.

Works best with the phone on a flat surface so nobody nudges a finger off.

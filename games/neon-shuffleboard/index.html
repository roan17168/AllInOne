How To Slide

• Press the puck at the bottom and drag backwards — a power arrow appears.
• Release to launch. Longer pull = more power; sideways drag adds angle.
• Zones score 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 points. Overshoot the back rail and the puck is dead.
• Knock rival pucks out of scoring zones. 4 pucks each, highest total wins.

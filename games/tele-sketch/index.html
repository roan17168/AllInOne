    30-Second Quick Start

    • 1

      Pick a secret word. Everyone does this privately at the start.

    • 2

      Draw what you're given. Pass the phone. The next player draws your word — no letters, no numbers.

    • 3

      Guess what you see. Pass again. The next player only sees the drawing and types a guess. Repeat until the chain runs out.

      ASTRONAUT → [drawing of a scuba diver] → SCUBA GUY → [drawing of a fish] → SALMON

    Golden rule: pass the phone face-down. The whole payoff is the finale.

Liar's Dice Rules

• Each player starts with 5 Secret Dice.
• 1s (Ones) are Wild (unless 1s are bid directly).
• Bid higher on quantity or face value across all hidden cups in the room.
• Tap "LIAR!" to challenge the previous bid. Loser forfeits a die and drinks 2 sips!

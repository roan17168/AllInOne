Irish Poker Rules

• Every player is dealt 4 face-down cards.
• Card 1: Call Red or Black (1 sip penalty).
• Card 2: Call Higher or Lower than Card 1 (2 sips penalty).
• Card 3: Call Inside or Outside Cards 1 & 2 (3 sips penalty).
• Card 4: Call Exact Suit (4 sips penalty).
• Correct call = Distribute the designated sips to any rivals!
